# EcoHaven AI Prompt Testing

## Prompt 1 — Basic Recommendation

### Prompt
Suggest three eco-friendly destinations in India.

### Example Output
The AI suggested destinations such as Sikkim, Kerala, and Uttarakhand, highlighting their natural environments and sustainable tourism opportunities.

---

## Prompt 2 — Detailed Eco-Tourism Recommendation

### Prompt
Recommend three eco-friendly destinations in India and briefly explain what makes each destination suitable for sustainable tourism.

### Example Output
The AI provided three destinations along with explanations related to nature conservation, local communities, eco-friendly stays, and responsible tourism.

---

## Prompt 3 — Personalized Recommendation

### Prompt
I want to visit India and prefer nature, sustainable homestays, local experiences, and responsible tourism. Suggest three destinations that would suit these preferences.

### Example Output
The AI recommended destinations based on nature, sustainable accommodation, local experiences, and responsible travel preferences.

---

## Best Prompt

Prompt 3 worked best because it provided the AI with more specific context about the user's preferences. This resulted in recommendations that were more relevant to eco-tourism and sustainable travel. The additional details helped the AI generate a more personalized response rather than a generic list of destinations.

## System Prompt / Role

The AI was instructed to act as an EcoHaven eco-tourism assistant and provide concise, useful recommendations focused on sustainable destinations, eco-friendly homestays, local experiences, and responsible tourism.
